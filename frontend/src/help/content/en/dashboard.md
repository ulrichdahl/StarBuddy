# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. It shows how your main org is doing, what you are working towards, and what is in the refinery.

![The dashboard](screens/web-dashboard.jpg)

## Org standings

The tiles at the top compare your main org with the other orgs. Pick a tile to switch the ranking: overall, missions, refinery or travel. `Leaderboard period` switches between this month and all time.

Points come only from the game's own log, sent by members' desktop clients. Nothing typed into the website scores.

| Event | Points |
| --- | --- |
| A contract completed | 10 |
| A refinery work order finished | 5 |
| A new place visited this month (Stanton, Nyx, Pyro) | 3 to 8 |
| A jurisdiction entered, once a month | 2 |

An org's score is its points divided by the pilots who scored, so a small active org can beat a large quiet one. The **In the app** column shows refinery orders and SCU recorded in StarBuddy for interest. It is not ranked, because those can be typed in by hand.

**Top members** shows who earned the most for your org. **Your points** shows what you earned.

## Your trackers

- **Blueprints:** reward pools you track, with the blueprints still missing. Track a pool from a blueprint's details on [Blueprints](blueprints).
- **Gathering:** your pinned craft targets and how much is left to gather. See [Gathering](gathering).
- **Refinery orders:** your open orders, what is ready and when the next one finishes. See [Refinery](refinery).

## Server alerts

When RSI announces maintenance or an outage, a banner appears at the top of every page with a countdown to the shutdown. Press the bell in the banner to get a browser notification for new notices.
