# Scan training

Scan training collects screenshots of in-game panels to teach StarBuddy to read them better. It is for testers: members with the tester role in the community's Discord. Others do not see it.

![Scan training](screens/web-training.jpg)

## Contribute a screenshot

1. Press `Choose screenshot` and pick a screenshot straight from the game: whole frame, no cropping, no HUD mods.
2. Click the four corners of the lit screen.
3. Pick which screen it is and the HUD colour. You can sample the colour from the picture.
4. Add the ship, and tick `Partly blocked` if something covers part of the panel.
5. Press `Submit for review`.

Awkward, off-angle and partly blocked captures are worth more than clean head-on ones: they are what real use looks like.

## Captures from the client

Press `F9` in the game and the desktop client sends the frame here, under **Captures**. Mark it up the same way.

**My submissions** shows what you sent and what the reviewers decided. **Coverage per screen** shows which screens still need pictures.
