# Organizations

An org pools what its members choose to share: org-visible materials and items, blueprints and refinery work. The dashboard ranks the orgs against each other.

![Organizations](screens/web-organizations.jpg)

Open your name in the top right corner and choose `Organizations`.

## Join an org

Press `Request to join` on the org's card. It shows **Pending approval** until a manager accepts you. The org's managers get a Discord message about your request, and you get one when you are accepted. You can be in more than one org.

An org is optional. Everything else in StarBuddy works for a solo player too.

## Your main org

If you are in several orgs, one is your **main org**: it gets your points and is the one the dashboard shows, as in Star Citizen. Press `Make main` on another org to change it.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Share stock and see the org's shared stock and standings. |
| Officer, manager, admin | Also clear the org's inventory on the [Admin](admin) page. |
| Manager, admin | Also accept and decline join requests, and kick members. |

## Manage members

Managers get a Discord direct message when someone asks to join, and the dashboard says how many are waiting, with `Review`. Press it, or `Manage members`, to accept or decline requests and to kick members. The list also shows how much each member shares.

## Leave an org

Press `Leave`. If you are the org's last manager, you pick a member to take over first.

## New orgs

An admin of the community's Discord server creates orgs with `/starbuddy org create` in Discord, and appoints their first manager with `/starbuddy org manager`. Ask in the Discord if your org is missing.
