# Items

The Items page holds your components, weapons, armour and other gear: crafted, bought or looted.

![Items](screens/web-items.jpg)

## Add items

Press `Add items`. The sheet works as on [Materials](materials): a location for the whole sheet, then one line per stack.

- **Item:** search by name or class. If the catalogue does not know it, `Enter` keeps what you typed as the class.
- **Amount** and **visibility**, as for materials.
- **Quality** is optional, for crafted or bought gear.

## Crafted items

An item you recorded with `I crafted this` on the [Craft](craft) page shows as **crafted**. The undo button on it removes the item, puts the materials back and rolls back the blueprint use.

## Views, moving and selling

The **Stacks** and **Org** views, `Only mine`, and `Move, share or sell` work as on [Materials](materials).
