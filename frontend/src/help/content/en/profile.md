# Profile

Open your name in the top right corner and choose `Profile`.

![Profile](screens/web-profile.jpg)

## Identity

- **Star Citizen handle:** your in-game handle. Org mates see it, CSV imports use it to match rows to you, and the desktop client uses it to know which character is yours. Only game events from your own characters score points for your org.
- **Avatar:** comes from Discord.

## Desktop client

The desktop client, your paired devices and the ship names and game settings it installs have their own page: press `Desktop client` in the top bar. See [Install and pair the client](client-setup) and [StarLabels and StarConfig](starlabels).

## Your stock

After a game wipe, clear what the patch took from your own stock:

1. Press `Clear my stock…`.
2. Untick the material categories that survived. Tick `Items` only if the patch took items too.
3. Type `CLEAR` and confirm.

This removes your private and org-visible stacks alike, and never anyone else's. It cannot be undone.
