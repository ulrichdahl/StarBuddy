# The client's pages

The client's pages show the same data as the website, so you can look things up without leaving the game. They need the client to be [paired](client-setup).

![Craft in the client](screens/client-craft.jpg)

- **Craft:** what you can craft now from your blueprints and what you hold. `Craft` records a craft, as on the website's [Craft](craft) page, and `Undo` takes it back.
- **Blueprints:** the same checklist as on the website, in the same order and with the same filters and `Mark all shown as owned`. Each row shows its reward pool and how much of the pool you have. At the far right, the eye tracks the pool and the pin makes the blueprint a craft target. `↑` `↓` and `Space` tick rows while you walk the fabricator in the game. See [Blueprints](blueprints).
- **Gathering:** your shopping list for the pinned craft targets. Post it to Discord from here. See [Gathering](gathering).
- **Refinery:** your refinery orders with the time left. `Collect` an order that is ready and say where it went. See [Refinery](refinery).
- **My stash:** your items, and the items the org shares.
- **Org:** your main org's members, who is playing now, where and in which ship. Only members who share their game status show it.
- **Tools:** your org's website pages, community tools (UEX, Regolith, Erkul, the wiki, Cornerstone, SC Trade Tools) and your own links. Also where UnitedDanes StarLabels and StarConfig are installed. See [StarLabels and StarConfig](starlabels).
