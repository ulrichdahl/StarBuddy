# Getting started

StarBuddy keeps track of what you and your org have in Star Citizen: the materials and items you hold, the blueprints you own, and your refinery orders. From that it works out what the org can craft, and what is still worth mining. A desktop client reads the game's log and parts of the game screen, so there is less to type.

![The sign-in page](screens/web-login.jpg)

## 1. Sign in with Discord

Press `Sign in with Discord` and accept. StarBuddy only lets in members of its community's Discord server. If the sign-in page says your account is not a member, join the Discord server first, then sign in again.

StarBuddy stores your Discord id, name and avatar, and nothing else from Discord.

## 2. Set your Star Citizen handle

Open your name in the top right corner and choose `Profile & desktop client`. Type your in-game handle under **Identity**. Org mates see it, and the desktop client uses it to know which of the game's characters is yours. See [Profile and desktop client](profile).

## 3. Join your org

Choose `Organizations` in the same menu, find your org and press `Request to join`. A manager of the org accepts you. Until then the org's shared stock and standings stay hidden. See [Organizations](organizations).

## 4. Add what you have

- Materials go on the [Materials](materials) page, items on [Items](items).
- Tick the blueprints you own on [Blueprints](blueprints).
- Record refinery work orders on [Refinery](refinery), or let the desktop client read them for you.

Each stack is **private** (only you) or **org-visible** (your org can see it and craft with it). You choose per stack and can change it later.

## 5. Install the desktop client (optional, recommended)

The client sends your new blueprints, finished missions and refinery jobs to StarBuddy while you play, reads refinery orders and mining scans off the screen, and shows small windows over the game. See [Install and pair the client](client-setup).

## Getting around

- The menu on the left has every page. The button at its top folds it to icons.
- Press `/` to search for an item from anywhere.
- Press `?`, or the `?` next to a page's title, to open the help for the page you are on. It slides in from the right, so you can follow the steps while you read.
- The globe button switches between English and Danish. Your choice follows you to other devices.
