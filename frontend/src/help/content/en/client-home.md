# The client's Home page

Home is what the client opens on. It shows at a glance whether everything is running.

![The client's Home page](screens/client-home.jpg)

## The four status boxes

- **Servers:** the Star Citizen services, from RSI's status page. Amber with a countdown when maintenance is announced.
- **Game.log:** whether the log watcher is running, and how many events it has sent this session. `Start` and `Stop` it here.
- **Screen:** whether screen reading is on. Refinery reads and scanning need it. See [Screen reading](client-screen-reading).
- **Game status:** whether you share your game status with your main org: whether you play, where, in which ship and on which shard. It is off until you press `Share`. Turned off, the website forgets it at once.

## Below the boxes

- **Refinery:** the last refinery order the client read, with the time left. `Open` shows it in full.
- **Recent from Game.log:** what the game logged this session: blueprints, missions, refinery jobs and travel.
- **Windows over the game:** every overlay window. Tick one to open it over the game, untick it to close it. See [Windows over the game](client-overlays).

## The menu

On the left: Home, Craft, Blueprints, Gathering, Refinery, My stash, Org, Tools and Settings. See [The client's pages](client-pages).
