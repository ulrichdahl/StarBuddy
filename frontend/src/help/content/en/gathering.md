# Gathering

Gathering is your shopping list: what your pinned craft targets need, against what you hold at the right quality.

![Gathering](screens/web-gathering.jpg)

## Pin craft targets

Open a blueprint on [Blueprints](blueprints) or [Craft](craft) and choose `Pin as craft target`. It shows up here as a card. Use `+` and `−` on the card for how many you want, and unpin it when you are done.

## The shopping list

For every material your targets need:

- **Needed** and the **Quality** the recipe asks for.
- **You have (qualifying):** only stock at that quality or better counts, so you know what is worth mining. Stock below the quality is shown apart.
- **Org has:** with `Include org stock`, org-visible stock counts too.
- **Still to get:** what is left to mine or buy.

`Post shopping list to Discord` posts the list in your community's Discord, so others can help.

The desktop client shows the same list in its Gathering page and in a small window over the game.
