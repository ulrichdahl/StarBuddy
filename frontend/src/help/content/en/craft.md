# Craft

The Craft page shows what the org can craft with the materials on hand: the best options first, the nearest misses after.

![Craft](screens/web-craft.jpg)

## Read the list

- **Owners:** who owns the blueprint. You can only craft from a blueprint someone in the org owns.
- **Have materials:** the bar shows your private materials in teal, what the org can supply in amber, and what is still missing in dark.
- **Missing materials:** what is still needed, and how much.
- **Est. quality:** the quality the craft would come out at with the best stacks available.

Filters: `Craftable now`, `Include unowned blueprints`, `Pinned only`, type and search.

## Record a craft

Click a blueprint to open it.

![Recording a craft](screens/web-craft-detail.jpg)

1. Pick who is crafting it under **Blueprint owners**.
2. Set **How many**, and whether it is for personal or org use.
3. Pick the quality you want (`Low`, `Mid` or `High`). The stacks to draw from are ticked for you. Change the ticks if you used other stacks.
4. Craft it in the game, then press `I crafted this`.

The materials are taken from the stacks and the item lands on your [Items](items) page. `Undo` puts everything back.
