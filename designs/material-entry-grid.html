Material entry — spreadsheet grid

Assessment of the tester's multi-line entry idea against StarBuddy's fast-manual-input philosophy, with the refinements from 2026-08-31 built in: arrows are pure navigation, only the focused cell edits, Enter saves the line and moves to the next, Ctrl+Enter repeats the current line with the amount cleared, and Amount sits before Quality. Material is an autocomplete over the resource catalog; Quality is a plain select of that material's known bands. The first grid is live — click a cell and type.

Mixed entry is a wash — 45 against today's 44. The win is repeats: 78 against 96, with the hands never leaving the keyboard. Nearly all of it comes from not retyping a repeated material and quality. What the grid uniquely buys on top is seeing and correcting the whole batch before it is committed.

4
Still open
  • Column order disagrees with the list. The Materials table reads Material · Quality · Amount; this grid reads Material · Amount · Quality. Putting Amount second means the repeat flow only ever moves forward — Ctrl+Enter lands on Amount, and Quality, the other field that sometimes changes, is the next stop rather than a step backwards. The cost is that the entry grid and the list beneath it no longer line up.
  • Plain arrow stepping is gone. ±0,001 on the bare arrows was the finest step; only Ctrl (0,01) and Shift (0,1) survive. If 0,001 is used in practice, it needs another home.
  • Materials with no bands yet. Roughly a third of the catalog has an empty known_qualities — Antium, Carbon, Cobalt and the rest. Those rows fall back to a typed number, as the current form does. Try Antium in the grid to see it.
  • Per-row overrides. Location and Visibility move to the batch header. A row that differs needs an escape hatch — most likely editing it in the list after saving.
