Overlays — two directions

The two shortlisted ideas, each over a mock game viewport with the two first overlay windows: the RSI status maintenance alert (today's real "Live Deployment" notice) and the mining scan signature window. Both lean on the RSI Launcher look — dark glass, hairlines, one cyan accent, amber for warnings — and differ in how much screen they take. The scan window now follows the reference lookup entry: rock type and signature, match and mineral count, occurrence, then one row per mineral with chance, share range, resistance and quality band.

A Launcher Glass

The most faithful translation of the Launcher: wide frosted cards with a glowing accent edge, an eyebrow-and-title header strip, real body copy and a footer of actions — the overlay reads as a piece of the Launcher that followed you into the game.

  • Appear: 300 ms fade with an 8 px slide from the top; after 20 s idle the warning folds to its header strip (eyebrow, title, countdown) and re-expands on hover or click.
  • Click-through: the whole viewport passes input to the game except the cards themselves; Esc or Dismiss hides the alert until the incident posts a new update, which re-shows it.
  • Timing: the countdown ticks every second toward the incident's 1445 UTC; under five minutes it turns red and the CTA reads "Log off now".
  • Scan card: appears about 400 ms after the in-game scan completes and fades after 12 s, or immediately when a new scan replaces it. Rows of the same mineral are the distinct share bands the signature allows; the best-quality band sits on top.

B Signal Strip

  • Drain line: the amber line under the strip runs from the notice time (1415) to shutdown (1445), so remaining time reads at a glance even without the digits.
  • Click-through: the strip is the only hit-target; hovering it drops a detail panel with the full notice text, moving away closes it.
  • Collapse: the chevron folds the strip to a 4 px line carrying only the drain; the next status update re-opens it.
  • Rail: shows only while the player is in a mining seat and hides itself 15 s after the last scan. Each mineral row is two lines: share range on top, resistance / quality band / chance beneath, so the 260 px rail carries the whole entry without a table.
